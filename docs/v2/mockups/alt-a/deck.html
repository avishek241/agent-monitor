Agent Deck · Mission Control

Version A, phone deck at 390 px. A dark, dense command-centre look: monospace numerals, neon on charcoal, thin rules, status LEDs, sparklines for quota burn and a live event ticker.

  • The scope strip on top switches between ALL (the overview) and one project. On a project, OVR becomes the project page, and every other tab filters to that project.
  • WORKERS shows each account's 5-hour quota as a sparkline and a 20-cell bar, the time to reset, and its parallel lanes. The office account keeps its usage visible but is greyed out, with the reason.
  • BOARD shows column counts, open dependency edges and the Ready, Running, Review and Blocked lists.
  • REVIEW lets you approve or request changes. OPS holds Alerts, Timeline and Repo health.
  • The page fakes live updates: the clock, countdowns, row counts and new ticker events every 6 s.
