Notes for B3.
  • Data comes only from /api/overview, /api/projects/{p}, …/tasks/{id} and …/eligible-workers, plus SSE /api/events.
  • Scope badges come from the view models. The UI never infers them.
  • Disabled worker rows use allowed:false and show the server's reason verbatim.
  • Landscape and tablet: Overview becomes PhoneDeck's three-column grid (LCD + accounts | projects | needs-you).
  • An OFFLINE badge replaces the LED when SSE drops.
  • Lanes come from accounts[].lanes and accounts[].capacity. A free lane always shows the planner's reason: what size fits, or why nothing fits (domain, policy, cooling). Spare lanes with eligible work turn orange after spareAlertMin.
